KITA CLIPS 2.1

Install:   cd ~/Downloads/KitaClips && bash build.sh

OPEN IT
- Control+Option+V from anywhere. It opens near the top of whichever screen your mouse is on,
  so it works even when the menu bar icon is hidden behind the notch.
- Or click the clipboard icon in the menu bar. Right-click it for Pause, Open at Login, Quit.
- Opens by itself every time you restart (Open at Login is on from the first run).

USE
- Everything you copy is saved as a card: text, links, emails, numbers, code, colours,
  images (screenshots, pictures) and files from Finder.
- Type to search. Tab switches filters (All, Pinned, Text, Links, Code, Images, Files).
- Up/Down to move, Return to copy. Cmd+1..9 copy the first nine cards.
- Cmd+P pin, Cmd+Delete delete, Esc closes.
- Pinned cards stay on top and are never auto-deleted.
- Right-click a card: Copy as UPPERCASE / lowercase / Title Case / Sentence case,
  trim spaces, join into one line, remove empty lines. Open link, Show in Finder,
  Stop saving from this app.

PASTE STRAIGHT INTO THE APP (optional)
- Gear menu > Paste straight into the app. Then Return pastes into the app you were typing in.
- macOS asks for Accessibility permission the first time: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Accessibility > turn on Kita Clips. After a rebuild you may need to turn it off and on again.
- Shift+Return (or Option-click) does the opposite of this setting.

SETTINGS (gear)
- Keep up to 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 / 1000 clips (default 200)
- Delete after 1 / 7 / 30 / 90 days or never (default 30)
- Pause saving for 5 minutes, 1 hour or until you resume
- Save images, Save copied files, Ignored apps, Clear unpinned clips

PRIVACY
- Passwords from password managers (1Password, Bitwarden, Apple Passwords, Keychain, LastPass...)
  are never saved.
- History: ~/Library/Application Support/KitaClips/history.json, images in the images folder.
- Your clips from Kita Clips 1 are brought over the first time 2.0 runs.

NEW IN 2.1
- If the menu bar is too full and the icon hides behind the notch, Kita Clips tells you once
  and you can still open it with Control+Option+V or from the Clips tab in Notch Player.
- Works with Notch Player 3.6: your clips show in the notch.
