Save your own posts, or ones you have permission to use. Re-uploading other people's videos can get your account flagged for copyright.

How it works
  1. Open the post in TikTok or Instagram and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Come back here and tap Paste, then Get video.
  3. Pick what to save. TikTok videos come without the watermark.

On Android, install Kitatool from your browser menu ("Add to Home screen"). Then you can share a post straight to Kitatool.
