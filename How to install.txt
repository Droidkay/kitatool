NOTCH PLAYER 3.3 — HOW TO INSTALL

1. Quit the old Notch Player if it's running (menu bar icon > Quit Notch Player).
2. Drag "Notch Player.app" from this folder into your Applications folder.
   Choose Replace if it asks.
3. Open it from Applications.
   - "Apple could not verify..." -> click Done, then go to
     System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open.
   - "...is damaged and can't be opened" -> open Terminal, paste this, press Return:
       xattr -cr "/Applications/Notch Player.app"
     then open the app again.
4. When macOS asks to let Notch Player control Spotify or Music, click OK.

After this it opens by itself every time you restart or log in.
macOS may show a "Background item added" notice once; that's this.
To stop it, click the menu bar icon and untick Open at Login.
